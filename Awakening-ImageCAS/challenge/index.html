Ostia Challenge :)

Can you find the coronary ostia as accurately as our deep learning models?

The coronary ostia are the two points where the right and the left coronary arteries leave the aorta. You will scroll through 12 real cardiac CT scans and click where you think the right and the left ostium are. After every scan we show the expert reference and the predictions of our models, and your running statistics. Three more scans of a rare pediatric disease are mixed in near the end: those are not scored, they just help research.

  1. Scroll the two views (axial and sagittal) until you see the aortic root.
  2. Click the right ostium, then the left one. Refine each point in the second view.
  3. Submit and see how close you were, in millimetres.

Only your nickname, your clicks and your timing are stored, and results appear on a public leaderboard. No personal data is collected.
